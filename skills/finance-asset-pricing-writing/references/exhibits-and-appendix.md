# Asset-Pricing Exhibits and Appendix

## Core exhibits

- Sample/summary table with universe and missingness.
- Portfolio means showing all bins, long and short legs, spread, and units.
- Factor/pricing table with explicit benchmark models and comparable samples.
- Time plot or rolling estimate when stability is central.
- Holdout/postpublication table separated from discovery evidence.
- Cost/turnover/capacity exhibit for implementability claims.

## Figure notes

State formation date, holding period, overlapping observations, weights, rebalance frequency, annualization, benchmark, confidence interval construction, and recession/event shading source when used.

## Appendix map

Use the appendix for:

- exact signal/factor definitions and data timing;
- alternative breakpoints, weights, lags, and filters;
- complete model/factor comparisons;
- multiple-testing and search-space documentation;
- portfolio construction pseudo-code;
- cost assumptions and sensitivity;
- additional assets/countries/subperiods;
- proof or estimation detail for hybrid models.

Keep the central out-of-sample and implementation evidence in the main paper when it is required for the headline claim.
