# Corporate, Governance, Household, and International Archetypes

Read this reference when the paper's central contribution is non-causal corporate finance, governance, household finance, or cross-country evidence and none of the specialist skills owns the main claim. Use it to select an argument architecture, not to infer a journal preference or imitate an exemplar.

## Route before drafting

Classify the paper by the evidence that carries its main claim:

- Stay in the core skill for descriptive facts, conditional associations, institutional comparisons, surveys, decompositions, or mixed evidence without a dominant specialist design.
- Add the causal specialist when a shock, assignment rule, IV, RDD, DID, or experiment identifies the central effect.
- Add the asset-pricing specialist when expected returns, factor exposures, SDF restrictions, portfolio tests, or implementable return prediction carry the claim.
- Add the intermediation specialist when bank/nonbank balance sheets, credit supply, dealer behavior, liquidity, or market design is the central mechanism rather than the setting.
- Add the theory/structural specialist when equilibrium restrictions, estimation of primitives, counterfactuals, or welfare carry an independent claim.

Do not route from vocabulary alone. A loan outcome does not establish credit supply; a stock-return outcome does not make a governance paper an asset-pricing paper; a country reform does not establish causality without a valid comparison and assumptions.

## Corporate finance architecture

Use for financing, investment, payout, ownership, contracting, mergers, innovation, distress, or firm boundaries.

Build the paper around an economic margin:

1. Define the decision, constraint, or contracting friction.
2. Show the central empirical fact and its economically relevant benchmark.
3. Distinguish competing interpretations using timing, cross-sectional predictions, contract terms, quantities, prices, or real outcomes.
4. Trace incidence: identify which stakeholders gain, lose, substitute, or bear risk.
5. Bound the result by firm type, institutional setting, financing regime, or sample selection.

Keep firm policy and firm value distinct. An observed change in leverage, investment, disclosure, or payout is not automatically efficient, value increasing, or caused by the proposed friction. If valuation is used, state the measurement and interpretation assumptions.

## Governance architecture

Organize governance studies as `institution or allocation of control -> changed incentives/information -> corporate decision -> stakeholder outcome`. Define who holds formal rights, who exercises effective influence, and when each is measured.

Separate four objects that are often collapsed:

- governance rule or ownership structure;
- monitoring, voice, voting, engagement, or intervention;
- managerial or board response;
- firm, shareholder, employee, creditor, or social outcome.

Do not infer monitoring from ownership alone, engagement from a meeting count, or welfare from a market reaction alone. Address selection into ownership, proposals, votes, boards, engagements, or disclosure. When evidence is associational, present the mechanism as a disciplined interpretation and name viable alternatives.

## Household finance architecture

Use for participation, portfolio choice, borrowing, mortgages, insurance, retirement, advice, attention, beliefs, literacy, or financial distress.

Start with the household decision and feasible choice set. Then define information, constraints, prices, intermediaries, and institutional defaults. Report both participation/extensive-margin and conditional/intensive-margin outcomes when they answer different questions. Translate coefficients into household-relevant units such as dollars, payment changes, default probability, wealth share, or consumption exposure, while preserving the original estimand.

Distinguish preference, belief, attention, capability, access, and supply explanations. Administrative records reveal behavior but may not reveal beliefs; surveys reveal stated beliefs or preferences but introduce measurement and selection concerns; platform or adviser data may observe only selected users and endogenous product menus. Do not label a departure from a benchmark a mistake without stating the benchmark and required assumptions.

## International and cross-country architecture

Define the comparison before interpreting it. Record country coverage, period, institutional variation, currency and inflation treatment, accounting harmonization, market development, and sample-composition rules. Separate within-country, between-country, and global-shock variation; they answer different questions.

Use institutions as measured constructs, not country labels. Explain comparability and whether a proxy has the same economic meaning across legal, reporting, household, or market regimes. A country fixed effect does not solve time-varying confounding, and a broad cross-country association does not establish that transplanting one institution would reproduce the outcome elsewhere.

## Deliverable check

Before writing, record: main economic margin, evidence role, unit and population, closest alternative interpretations, incidence, and scope. In the introduction, state the empirical contribution at the supported rung of the claim ladder. In results, keep quantities, prices, choices, and welfare separate. In the conclusion, carry forward the same population and institutional boundary rather than generalizing from firms to economies, selected households to all households, or observed stakeholder outcomes to aggregate welfare.
