## 1. Core architecture

Build the introduction around this sequence:

```text
economic tension or consequential unknown
→ precise question
→ why current evidence is insufficient
→ approach and source of credibility
→ main findings with magnitudes
→ mechanisms/validation and limits
→ contribution relative to closest work
→ optional roadmap
```

Use this as a functional order, not a rigid paragraph count. Lead with the research problem; do not spend several paragraphs on generic importance before stating the question.

The order is defeasible. A measurement paper may define the object before the gap; a theory paper may state the empirical facts before the missing mechanism; a causal paper may need a short institutional event before it can make the counterfactual problem intelligible. Preserve the dependency among functions even when their surface order changes.

## 2. Give each paragraph one job

### Opening

- Establish a concrete tension, puzzle, or decision.
- State why resolving it changes an economic belief, measurement, or policy tradeoff.
- Reach the question quickly.

### Gap

- Identify what is not known and why—not merely that “few studies examine” the topic.
- Tie the gap to a limitation of evidence, theory, measurement, setting, or scope.
- Avoid attacking a broad literature when only the closest paper matters.

### Approach

- Name the setting/data/model.
- Explain the source of variation or theoretical mechanism in plain economic language.
- State the estimand or model object.
- Surface the main identifying assumptions and why the design helps.

### Findings

- Report the main result early with units and a meaningful benchmark.
- Organize secondary results by evidentiary role: validation, mechanism, boundary, or consequence.
- Give important nulls the role they deserve.
- Avoid walking through tables in order.

### Contribution

- Compare with the nearest papers on the exact margin changed.
- Distinguish contribution to fact, method/measurement, mechanism/theory, or scope.
- Explain why the new evidence changes interpretation rather than merely adding another setting.

## 3. Build contribution claims

Use a three-part test:

1. **Prior frontier**: what the closest verified work establishes.
2. **Binding limitation**: what remains unresolved and why.
3. **Current advance**: which evidence or model object overcomes that limitation and what conclusion changes.

Prefer `We provide evidence that...`, `We distinguish...`, or `We quantify...` to ceremonial `We contribute to three literatures`. Do not enumerate contributions unless the paper genuinely has separable advances.

Make the design and contribution interact: the contribution is not that a method was used, but that its leverage changes what can be inferred about the economic question. If the design identifies a local object, state the contribution at that scope.

## 4. Position the literature

Use concentric rings:

1. Direct competitors addressing the same question.
2. Papers sharing the mechanism or design.
3. Broader context only when needed to interpret stakes.

For every citation cluster, state the relationship: confirms, contradicts, extends, distinguishes, measures, or changes scope. A citation list without a logical verb is not positioning.

Default to integrating literature into the introduction. Create a separate literature section only if it synthesizes several debates, develops hypotheses, or explains technical distinctions that would derail the opening argument.

## 5. Common failures and repairs

| Failure | Repair |
|---|---|
| Generic opening | Replace with a concrete economic tension and decision consequence. |
| Gap by absence | Explain why existing designs/measurements cannot answer the question. |
| Design name as credibility | State the variation, comparison, assumptions, and threat. |
| Result list | Group evidence by the claim it supports. |
| Contribution list | Compare on an exact margin against closest work. |
| Causal overreach | Use association/prediction language or explain identifying assumptions. |
| Long roadmap | Keep only when the architecture is genuinely nonstandard. |
| Journal cosplay | Preserve the evidence chain; adapt headings, not facts or certainty. |
