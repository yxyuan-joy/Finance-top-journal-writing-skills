# Official Submission Sources

Checked: 2026-08-13. Treat every fee, deadline, file rule, AI policy, anonymity rule, and formatting limit as mutable. Reopen the live official source before advising on a real submission.

## The Journal of Finance

- [AFA: Submission to the Journal of Finance](https://afajof.org/submissions/) — current operational submission page.
- [AFA: Submission Guidelines and Policies PDF](https://afajof.org/wp-content/uploads/JF_Submission_Guidelines.pdf) — detailed writing and presentation guidance; the PDF identifies a 2024 revision date.
- [Wiley: The Journal of Finance, For Authors](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15406261/homepage/forauthors.html) — publisher-side author entry point.

Use the live AFA page for current operational rules. The guidelines PDF is especially relevant to introduction, citation discipline, equations/exhibits, and a brief nontechnical conclusion, but older fee/refund language may conflict with the live page.

## Journal of Financial Economics

- [Elsevier: JFE Guide for Authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-financial-economics/publish/guide-for-authors) — publisher policies, scope, ethics, and author guidance.
- [JFE editorial office: Submissions](https://www.jfinec.com/submissions) — operational submission checklist and editorial-office requirements.
- [JFE editorial office: Accepted Paper Instructions](https://www.jfinec.com/accepted-paper-instructions) — post-acceptance production and data/code instructions only.

Do not use accepted-paper instructions as initial-submission requirements. If the Elsevier template and editorial-office page appear inconsistent, flag the conflict and advise contacting the office.

## The Review of Financial Studies

- [SFS: Submit a Paper](https://sfs.org/review-of-financial-studies/submit-a-paper/) — current initial-submission and revision policies.
- [SFS: Submission Checklist](https://sfs.org/review-of-financial-studies/submit-a-paper/submit-a-paper-format/) — manuscript-format checklist.
- [OUP: RFS Instructions to Authors](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/pages/Instructions_To_Authors) — journal scope and production-oriented author instructions.

Use the SFS pages for initial submission. Use OUP instructions for accepted-manuscript production details, while checking whether any dated style or media specification has changed.

## How to answer compliance questions

1. Open the relevant live official page.
2. State the checked date.
3. Separate `initial submission`, `revision`, and `accepted manuscript` rules.
4. Quote only what is necessary and paraphrase the rest.
5. Identify conflicts or ambiguity rather than choosing silently.
6. Never infer a hard rule from the structure of published papers.
