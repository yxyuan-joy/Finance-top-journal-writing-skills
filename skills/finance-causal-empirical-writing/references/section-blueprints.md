# Causal-Empirical Section Blueprints

## Introduction paragraph jobs

1. Economic decision/tension and causal question.
2. Why existing comparisons are confounded or incomplete.
3. Institutional event and assignment/timing.
4. Estimand, treated/comparison units, and identifying assumption.
5. Main estimate with units and baseline benchmark.
6. Design diagnostics and remaining boundary.
7. Mechanism/consequence evidence.
8. Exact contribution relative to closest designs/questions.

## Institutional section

Use a design-centered timeline:

```text
pre-period and eligibility measurement
→ assignment/announcement
→ anticipation window
→ implementation/compliance
→ outcome measurement
→ possible spillovers or subsequent policies
```

## Design section

1. Define sample and treatment.
2. Define estimand/counterfactual.
3. Present equation and coefficient of interest.
4. Explain identifying variation.
5. State assumptions.
6. Explain controls/fixed effects/weights/inference.
7. Pre-state diagnostics and threats.

## Results order

1. Treatment assignment/first stage or exposure validation.
2. Main effect and economic magnitude.
3. Dynamics and design diagnostics.
4. Threat-targeted robustness.
5. Mechanism and heterogeneity.
6. Downstream/equilibrium consequences and external validity.

## Conclusion

State the identified effect for the correct population and setting, the key assumption/boundary, and the economic implication. Avoid universal policy prescriptions when the design identifies a local or short-run effect.
