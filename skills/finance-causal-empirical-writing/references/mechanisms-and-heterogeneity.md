# Mechanisms, Heterogeneity, and Mediation

## Mechanism ladder

1. **Consistency**: the main result matches a channel's qualitative prediction.
2. **Intermediate outcome**: treatment changes a variable on the proposed path.
3. **Cross-sectional prediction**: effects vary with a pre-treatment channel proxy.
4. **Timing**: intermediate and final outcomes move in the predicted sequence.
5. **Competing-channel discrimination**: evidence differs under plausible alternatives.
6. **Mediation/structural decomposition**: channel share is identified under additional assumptions.

Use wording at the achieved rung. Do not call rung 1 proof.

## Heterogeneity rules

- Motivate splits before reporting them.
- Use pre-treatment variables when claiming channel evidence.
- Test differences across groups rather than comparing separate significance stars.
- Account for multiple subgroup searches.
- Report group sizes, baseline outcomes, and support/overlap.
- Distinguish economically meaningful heterogeneity from scale artifacts.

## Mediation cautions

Adding a post-treatment mediator can induce bias. State the assumptions required for a mediation estimate and distinguish descriptive coefficient attenuation from an identified indirect effect.

## Alternative explanations

For each alternative:

1. State its predicted timing, subgroup, intermediate outcome, or auxiliary consequence.
2. Explain the test's power and measurement quality.
3. Report whether evidence rejects, weakens, or remains compatible with the alternative.
4. Preserve residual ambiguity.
