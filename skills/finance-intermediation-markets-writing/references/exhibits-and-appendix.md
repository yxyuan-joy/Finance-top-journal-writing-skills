# Intermediation and Markets Exhibits

## Useful main-paper objects

- institutional timeline or flow diagram;
- sample/entity-linkage table;
- balance-sheet or order-flow measurement validation;
- price and quantity results side by side;
- event/dynamic plot around funding, rule, or market change;
- channel-discrimination table;
- substitution/spillover/aggregate-incidence table;
- network visualization only when layout conveys exposure or propagation more clearly than a table.

## Required notes

State the side of market, entity level, aggregation window, timestamp convention, currency/basis points/percent units, winsorization, weighting, fixed effects, standard-error level, and whether quotes/orders are posted, routed, executed, canceled, or filled.

## Appendix

Use for contract/rule detail, data dictionaries, entity matching, quote–trade algorithms, alternative liquidity measures, balance-sheet mappings, network construction, additional substitution markets, and welfare/model sensitivity. Keep the evidence required to distinguish the headline mechanism in the main text.
