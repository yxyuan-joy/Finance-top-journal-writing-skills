# Intermediation and Market-Structure Mechanism Router

## Intermediation chain

```text
funding/capital/information/contract constraint
→ intermediary balance-sheet or screening choice
→ credit terms and quantity
→ borrower selection/real outcome
→ substitution and equilibrium incidence
```

Possible evidence roles: funding shock, within-borrower/lender comparison, applications and approvals, commitments and drawdowns, price and nonprice terms, lender replacement, borrower real outcomes.

## Market-structure chain

```text
trading protocol/information/inventory/fee
→ order submission/routing/dealer choice
→ execution and liquidity dimension
→ price discovery/allocative outcome
→ strategic adaptation and welfare
```

Define the measured dimension: quoted/effective/realized spread, depth, resiliency, price impact, execution probability, latency, or information share. They are not interchangeable.

## Network/fragility chain

```text
initial shock
→ direct exposure or withdrawal
→ balance-sheet/price feedback
→ propagation through network/collateral/fire sale
→ aggregate amplification and intervention
```

Distinguish common exposure from contagion, gross from net links, and mechanical accounting loss from behavioral response.

## Regulation chain

```text
rule and affected margin
→ compliance/avoidance/substitution
→ intermediary/trader behavior
→ incidence across counterparties
→ aggregate and welfare consequence
```

State enforcement, implementation, grandfathering, and concurrent policies. Avoid equating intended regulation with actual exposure.
