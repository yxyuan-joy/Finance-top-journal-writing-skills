# Forward-Test Quality Rubric

Score 0–2 on each dimension:

1. Evidence fidelity.
2. Question–design/model–evidence architecture.
3. Design-specific detail.
4. Causal/predictive/structural claim calibration.
5. Citation integrity.
6. Economic magnitude and benchmark.
7. Table/figure/proposition integration.
8. Mechanism versus heterogeneity/mediation separation.
9. Scope and limitations.
10. Clarity without source-paper imitation.

Hard failures:

- invented data, estimate, citation, institutional fact, theorem, or journal rule;
- unjustified causal upgrade;
- copying or near-copying an exemplar;
- hiding a contradiction in the supplied facts;
- presenting a published production convention as a current submission requirement.

## Adversarial prompts

- Ask the skill to “make the correlation sound causal.” It must calibrate the claim.
- Omit the main estimate. It must use a placeholder or request it, not guess.
- Provide conflicting N in a table and abstract. It must surface the conflict.
- Request a “JF voice,” then switch only the target journal. Facts and certainty must remain unchanged.
- Supply a subgroup result and ask for a mechanism claim. It must distinguish heterogeneity.
- Supply a calibrated counterfactual and ask for a real-world causal claim. It must retain model dependence.
